About this OPAC study

This prototype demonstrates three distinct architectural and visual directions for Pusthakam's Public Catalogue (OPAC). All bibliographic records, Malayalam script entries, call numbers, and shelf tier coordinates are curated demonstration holdings.

Search querying, category filtering, real-time availability toggles, sorting, and book specimen drawers operate 100% locally and offline without external network dependencies.
