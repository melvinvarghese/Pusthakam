Questions

Straight answers about cost, data, equipment and moving from another system.

What does it cost?

Pricing will be announced with the first release. There will be no subscription, and no limits on how many books or members you can have.

Does it need the internet?

No. Cataloguing, circulation, members, reports, printing and backups all work without a connection. When the computer is online, Pusthakam looks up book covers by ISBN from Open Library; a setting to turn this off is planned. The Online Tools menu only opens links in your web browser.

Where is our data kept?

In one SQLite database file in the normal app-data folder on the library computer. Your records are never uploaded and no usage data is collected. The only thing sent out is a book's ISBN, when looking up its cover. Make regular backups and keep a copy on another drive.

Can we use our barcode scanner and printer?

Any USB barcode scanner that types like a keyboard should work, with nothing to configure. Labels, member cards, due slips and reports print through your normal printer dialog. We are still testing with real scanners and label printers, so please tell us how yours works.

Can we move our records from Koha or Excel?

Yes. Pusthakam imports catalogue records from Excel, CSV, MARC21 (ISO 2709, UTF-8) and MARCXML, with a ready-made profile for Koha exports, including item details. It can also import Koha patrons, current loans, loan history, reservations and fines. You see a preview with warnings and duplicates before anything is saved. Testing with a real Koha export is in progress.

How do backups work?

Make a backup from the Database Backups screen at any time, and switch on a daily automatic backup. Backups are checked after saving and can be copied to a USB drive. Restoring takes a safety copy of your current data first. Today only the most recent daily backup is kept; keeping a week, a month and a year of backups is planned before the first release.

Can several people use it at once?

Yes, over your local Wi-Fi or wired network. One library computer hosts the data. Readers can search the public catalogue from their phones, and staff can sign in from other devices in a web browser. The host computer must be on. Each staff member signs in with their own account.

What about closed days and fines?

Set your weekly closed days and holidays in the library calendar. A due date that falls on a closed day moves to the next open day. Fines follow your loan rules, with a grace period and an optional maximum.

Which languages is Pusthakam available in?

The app is in English today. A Malayalam version of the interface is being added, starting with the setup screens. Your book records can be in any language, including Malayalam, and search works for them too.

Does it run on Mac or Linux?

The first release will be for Windows. Pusthakam is built to run on macOS and Linux too, and those versions will follow once they have been tested.
