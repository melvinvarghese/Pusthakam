Status

An honest picture of where Pusthakam stands. Updated 27 November 2026, version 0.1.

Built, with automated tests

  • Catalogue, search and book covers
  • Accession register
  • Circulation: issue, return, renew, reservations, fines
  • Library calendar and loan rules
  • Members, member cards and photos
  • Import from Excel, CSV, MARC21 and MARCXML, with a Koha profile
  • Classification: Dewey, UDC, LC and custom
  • Inventory stock checks
  • Periodicals and acquisitions
  • Reports with CSV, Excel and PDF export
  • Barcode labels, spine labels and due slips
  • Staff accounts, roles and audit log
  • Backup and restore
  • Public catalogue and staff pages over Wi-Fi

Being tested in the real world

  • Installing on a fresh Windows computer
  • Real USB barcode scanners
  • Label printers, label sheets and member cards
  • Importing a real Koha export
  • Phones and tablets, including iPhone
  • The host computer sleeping or losing Wi-Fi
  • Large libraries of 10,000+ books
  • macOS and Linux builds

To do before the first release

  • Keep 7 daily, 4 weekly and 12 monthly backups, with an optional second backup folder
  • Secure staff sign-in over Wi-Fi
  • A setting to turn off online cover lookups
  • A signed Windows installer and published releases
  • Licence terms for libraries
  • A short, accurate guide for librarians
  • Malayalam interface (in progress)
