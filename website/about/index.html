About Pusthakam

Pusthakam (പുസ്തകം) is the Malayalam word for book.

Why it exists

Many libraries run on a register, a spreadsheet, or software that is expensive, needs a server or stops working when the internet does. Pusthakam is meant for the librarian who runs the desk, often alone: one program that covers the whole library, keeps every record on the library's own computer, and does not need a subscription.

It is designed around a few rules. The desk comes first, so everything works from the keyboard and a barcode scanner. Records are never lost or silently deleted. And the software is honest about what it can do: anything not finished is clearly marked as not available yet.

Who makes it

Pusthakam is created by Melvin Varghese. Work began in November 2026.

Become a pilot library

We are looking for two or three libraries to try Pusthakam alongside their current system before the first release, and tell us what works and what does not. Any kind of library is welcome: school, college, public, office or private collection.

To take part, open an issue on GitHub with the type and size of your library, the system you use today and the computers you have.

Report a problem or suggest an idea

Use GitHub issues. Please never include member names or other personal details from your library.
