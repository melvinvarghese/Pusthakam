# Open Folio

Status: concept development, not an approved identity. The existing PUS/THK spine-label mark was not supplied or approved as a final logo by the owner. The owner requested a new logo and a more appropriate splash-screen closing line.

A book becomes recognizable through its spine, fold and opening. Open Folio reduces those relationships to a compact silhouette: one upright spine, an open counter and a restrained turn of a page. The gesture should suggest a P without requiring lettering or explanation. The subject comes from the work of a library, not from a decorative stack of clip-art books.

Use generous negative space and broad, optically balanced forms. The mark must survive at the size of a navigation icon; small letter fragments, fine rules and interior ornament do not belong here. Refinement should come from carefully adjusted curves, spacing and weight rather than extra symbols. The final decision needs small-size and monochrome inspection, not just a large presentation.

Shelf ink, technology teal and light paper connect the concept to Pusthakam's existing interface. Color separates the spine and page rather than creating a gradient or a material effect. The silhouette should remain legible against the application's dark frame and light work surfaces. Preserve the calm character of the product and avoid glowing effects, shadows, badges or technical symbolism.

The name Pusthakam remains a separate readable wordmark. The symbol should not abbreviate it to PUS/THK. Its role is recognition, while typography supplies the name and the established tagline. For the splash screen, replace the technical Local Sovereign Architecture / Offline Ready badge with the plain line: More time for books and readers. Keep A Better Way to Manage Libraries. as the main tagline.

The image concept is a review artifact, not evidence of trademark clearance, ownership clearance or an approved final production logo. Installation icons and website assets should be updated only after the direction is accepted and the final asset is suitable at their required sizes.
