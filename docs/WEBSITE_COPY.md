## Product introduction

### Everything begins with a well-organised collection.

Pusthakam is being built around the real work of libraries: describing resources, tracking individual copies, managing members, circulating materials and producing the records librarians need.

It is designed for different kinds of libraries — from schools and colleges to public, special, research and private collections — using one configurable system.

## Feature sections

### Catalogue and discovery
Create and maintain bibliographic records with contributors, subjects, identifiers and classification. Find records with fast, full-text search.

### Accession and copy control
Track every physical copy with its own accession number, barcode, call number, collection and shelf location.

### Import existing records
Bring catalogue data into Pusthakam through CSV and MARC/ISO 2709 workflows. Preview and review records before committing an import.

### Circulation and membership
Member management and circulation workflows include issue, return, renewal and reservations. Automated tests cover these workflows. Native Windows verification remains pending.

### Reports and printing
Library reports, barcode labels and other print workflows are being developed in stages.

## Offline by design

### Your library should not depend on someone else's server.

Pusthakam is designed to keep core library operations on the computer where the application is installed. No cloud database, mandatory online account, recurring subscription or telemetry is required by the product plan.

Your catalogue is your library's working record. Local storage and dependable backup/restore are central design requirements.

## Development status

Pusthakam is actively being developed. Catalogue, accession, copy management, search and initial import workflows have been implemented and automated-tested.
